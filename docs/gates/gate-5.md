Run on 2026-11-03 (late evening) on the reference Mac (Apple M1 Max, 32 GB, macOS 27, Electron 45.2.1, iii 0.25.0, Ollama via Homebrew with `nomic-embed-text`, `qwen2.5:7b`, `qwen2.5vl:7b`), against an isolated copy of the development vault (22 artifacts: a Wikipedia article of 349 fragments, PDFs, scanned PDF, image, audio, video, text, HTML, `.eml`), driven by a second app instance (`PIM_WORKER_NAME=gate`) over the DevTools protocol, with the vault inspected read-only in SQLite.

## What was run

1. The enrich stage over the whole vault copy (captions, embeddings, schema extraction), then the link stage.
2. The vault checks in the table below (one SQL query each).
3. A click on "accept" on a model summary in the Item screen (the only way anything model-made becomes accepted).
4. The embedding model switched in Settings to `mxbai-embed-large` and one item enriched again (PRS-5).
5. Ollama stopped with `brew services stop ollama`: search, a new capture and the enrich stage observed; Ollama restarted and the skipped job observed (PRS-8).
6. Unit tests: 22 across vault, contracts and the model worker (`npm test`), including chunking, validation that drops foreign evidence ids and rejects malformed answers, and merging by normalised name.

## Found and fixed during the gate

- The 7B model ran away on one chunk of the article (over 11,000 generated tokens until the 10-minute cap, three attempts). Fix: `num_predict` capped at 2048, the per-chunk schema bounds every list, chunks of 4,000 characters.
- The result schema applied the per-chunk bounds to the merged answer, so a 27-chunk document was refused by the core three times (nothing was committed, as PRS-6 demands). Fix: bounds per chunk only; the merge keeps the best-evidenced 40 entities, 10 tags and 20 dates.
- The new link generators were too eager on this vault (217 shared-entity and 176 semantic proposals): shared model tags that are only proposed no longer count, an accepted entity on both ends counts 1 and a proposed non-tag entity 0.5, with a threshold of 1; semantic neighbours use 5 nearest at cosine distance under 0.3.
- Runs left `running` by an instance that quit mid-stage are now failed as `interrupted` on start.
- Text captures never queued extraction (since M1); they were only extracted by the backfill at the next start. Fixed.
- A second instance on the machine could not register (worker name taken): `PIM_WORKER_NAME` names it, and only the primary registers the kernel functions and the capture endpoint.

## Not verified by automation

The Inbox's bulk review of model suggestions with the keyboard; captions on video keyframes (the vault copy's video had its keyframes captioned once, 1 caption fragment counted, the UI not checked); the first-run detection text.

## Deviations

The worker receives fragment texts in the request rather than fetching them through the core; captions are fragments of the raw payload under `enrich::caption`; the cleaned title stays on the note. Ollama and its models are installed outside `infra/setup.sh`.
